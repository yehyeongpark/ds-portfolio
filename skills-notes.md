Data Science Job Skills Notes

Posting 1: Data Analyst
- Seniority Level: Entry to Mid-Level
- Industry: Retail & Athletic Footwear/Apparel
- Required Qualifications:
  - Advanced SQL proficiency for relational database queries and data joining
  - Experience building interactive dashboards and reports using Tableau or Power BI
  - Strong communication and presentation skills to convey insights to non-technical stakeholders
  - Understanding of retail KPIs, demand forecasting, and inventory optimization
  - Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or Business Analytics
- Preferred Qualifications:
  - Scripting skills in Python or R for exploratory data analysis
  - Familiarity with cloud data warehousing (Snowflake, BigQuery)
- Hidden Skills:
  - Cross-functional collaboration across supply chain planning and marketing
  - Business domain intuition regarding retail trends and product seasonality

 Posting 2: Associate Data Scientist
- Seniority Level: Associate / Early Career
- Industry: Fashion-Tech & E-commerce Personalization
- Required Qualifications:
  - Proficiency in Python (pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn) for statistical modeling and algorithm development
  - Strong SQL skills for database schema querying and feature extraction
  - Solid understanding of machine learning algorithms, recommender systems, and ranking models
  - Ability to communicate model decisions and experimental trade-offs to product teams
  - Quantitative degree (Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, or related)
- Preferred Qualifications:
  - Experience deploying ML models to cloud environments (AWS, GCP)
  - Practical knowledge of A/B testing design and hypothesis testing
  - Version control workflows using Git and GitHub
- Hidden Skills:
  - Handling subjective, noisy consumer preference data
  - Balancing theoretical modeling rigor with engineering delivery constraints

Overlapping Skills
- Advanced SQL querying for extracting and transforming data
- Clear communication of complex technical findings to non-technical audiences
- Foundational quantitative and statistical background
- Translating raw data into actionable business value

Reflection
What stood out most to me was the heavy emphasis both postings placed on interpersonal communication and stakeholder collaboration. I initially expected the Data Scientist role to focus almost purely on algorithmic engineering, but explaining model trade-offs to non-technical teams was just as essential as building the models. This highlights that communication is a core competency across the entire data science field rather than just an analyst requirement.
